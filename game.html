Katana World

A samurai tag-battle across 15 realms. Freeze your rivals, rescue your teammates, outlast the clock.

No ads No in-app purchases No free-text chat iPad & iPhone

How a round works

Two teams face off in one of fifteen realms. Swing your katana to freeze a rival; run to a frozen teammate to bring them back. When the clock runs out, the team still standing takes the round. Rounds are short, nobody is knocked out of the game, and a new one starts straight after.

What is in it

  • 15 distinct realms to fight across
  • Online team matches, plus Play Solo against AI opponents
  • A free-roam Demo mode for exploring a realm with no opponents at all
  • Simple touch controls - joystick, jump, attack - learned in a single round
  • Game Center leaderboards and achievements

What is deliberately not in it

  • No advertising of any kind, and no advertising SDKs
  • No in-app purchases and no subscriptions. One download, the whole game
  • No free-text chat, no voice chat, no photo sharing. Players can send only a fixed set of preset quick-chat phrases and emotes
  • No accounts and no sign-up. Nothing to register, nothing to remember
  • No third-party analytics or tracking

Privacy

Katana World collects no personal information. Online multiplayer uses Epic Online Services and Apple Game Center purely to put players into the same match. The full policy, including children's privacy, parental rights and how to request deletion, is on the support and privacy page.
