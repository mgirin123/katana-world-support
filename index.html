Katana World - Support

A stylized samurai tag-battle built for iPad, suitable for ages 9 and up. Two teams face off across 15 realms - swing your katana to freeze rivals, rescue your frozen teammates, and outlast the other team when the clock runs out.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

What we collect

Katana World collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no email collection, no advertising identifiers, no advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics. We do not build profiles, we do not track players across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share data with anyone for advertising purposes.

What the online features need in order to work

Online multiplayer is the one place where data leaves the device, and it is used only to put players into the same match:

ServiceWhat it handlesWhy
Epic Online Services A temporary session and device identifier, plus the network address needed to connect players Matchmaking and keeping a match in sync. Not used for advertising or tracking.
Apple Game Center The player's Game Center nickname and scores or achievements, handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy Leaderboards, achievements and finding other players. Visible to other players as the nickname only.

These identifiers are short-lived and are not stored by us after a match ends. We keep no server-side player records.

Communication between players

There is no free-text chat, no voice chat, and no way to send photos or links. Players can only send a fixed set of preset, pre-moderated quick-chat phrases and emotes chosen by us. Nothing a player types can reach another player.

Advertising and purchases

No ads. No in-app purchases. No external links inside the game.

Children's privacy

Katana World is designed to be safe for younger players and is built to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar rules elsewhere.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.
  • There is no mechanism in the game through which a child could disclose personal information - no free text, no profile, no photo upload, no email field.
  • No behavioural advertising, no third-party analytics, and no data sharing for marketing.
  • Game Center is controlled by Apple and can be limited or switched off entirely in iOS Settings, or through Screen Time and Content & Privacy Restrictions.

Parents and guardians may contact us at max.girin@gmail.com to ask what data exists for a player, to request its deletion, or to ask that online multiplayer be disabled. We will respond within 30 days. Because we hold no account records, deletion is usually a matter of confirming that nothing is stored.

Data retention and deletion

We store no personal data, so there is nothing for us to retain. Match session identifiers are discarded when the match ends. To request deletion of anything held by Apple Game Center, use Apple's own privacy tools; for Epic Online Services, see Epic's privacy policy.
