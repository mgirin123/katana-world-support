Katana World - Support

A stylized samurai tag-battle built for iPad, for kids 8-12. Two teams face off across 15 realms - swing your katana to freeze rivals, rescue your frozen teammates, and outlast the other team when the clock runs out.

Privacy Policy

Katana World is built to be safe for kids:

  • No personal information is collected. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no email collection, no advertising identifiers.
  • No chat with other players - only preset, kid-safe quick-chat phrases and emotes.
  • No ads.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No external links inside the game.
  • Online multiplayer uses Epic Online Services and Apple Game Center solely to connect players to matches - no personal data is shared with us or any third party for advertising or tracking purposes.
